胡佩珊醫師（Kate Woo）是香港註冊中醫師（註冊編號：008823），畢業於香港浸會大學中醫藥及生物醫學學士（一級榮譽）及輔導學社會科學碩士，並持有中醫臨床婦科學深造證書。她專注女性健康，提倡「身心同治」——診症時不只處理單一症狀，也會了解月經、睡眠、情緒、壓力與生活節奏，從整體狀態判斷體質與病機，以溫和、細膩、可持續的方式制定個人化調理方案。

Dr. Kate Woo (胡佩珊) is a registered Chinese medicine practitioner in Hong Kong (Reg. No. 008823), holding a BSc in Chinese Medicine and Biomedical Science (First Class Honours) and an MSocSc in Counselling from Hong Kong Baptist University, plus an Advanced Certificate in Clinical Gynecology. She focuses on women’s health with a mind-body approach — looking beyond a single symptom to the menstrual cycle, sleep, mood, stress and daily rhythm, and building gentle, sustainable, personalised treatment plans.
